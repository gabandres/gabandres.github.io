Callbook

You have an invitation

Callbook keeps a private record of return visits, bible studies and service hours. It is free, and it opens only for someone who was invited — which you were.

Install the app first, then open your invitation link again.

Get Callbook on the App Store

  1. Install Callbook from the App Store.
  2. Come back to the message you were sent and tap the invitation link again — the app will open and fill itself in.
  3. Choose a passphrase. That encrypts everything on your own device, and only you have it.

If tapping the link does nothing

Open Callbook, tap Get started, and paste this code into the invitation field.

Invitations have to be opened within two weeks of being sent. If yours has expired, ask whoever invited you for another — it takes them a moment.
